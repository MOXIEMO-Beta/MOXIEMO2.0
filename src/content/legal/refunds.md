# Refund Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 · Last updated October 29, 2026 · Version 1.0

## The short version

- New paid members can ask for a full refund of their first payment within 30 days of it. No questions asked.
- You can cancel anytime. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, and you are not charged again.
- Renewals are not refundable, except for mistakes on our side, such as a double charge.
- Email hello@buildwithmoxiemo.com from your account email. We reply within 3 business days.

## 1. The 30-day guarantee

If you are a new paid member, you can ask for a full refund of your first payment within 30 days of that payment. This covers your first monthly payment or your first annual payment, on any plan. You do not need to give a reason.

The guarantee is for your first paid subscription. It applies once per customer.

When we refund your first payment, your subscription ends and your paid access stops. Your strategy is yours to keep either way, so download anything you want before you ask. Your answers stay saved in case you come back, unless you ask us to delete them.

## 2. Cancelling

You can cancel anytime from your account settings, or by emailing hello@buildwithmoxiemo.com. Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and you are not charged again.

We do not refund the unused part of a month or year when you cancel, except as Sections 1 and 4 say.

## 3. Renewals and annual plans

Monthly and annual plans renew automatically until you cancel. Renewal payments are not covered by the 30-day guarantee.

If your first payment is for an annual plan, the guarantee covers it for 30 days. After that, we do not refund the unused months of an annual plan. We email you before an annual plan renews, so you have time to cancel if you want to.

## 4. Mistakes on our side

If we charge you by mistake, we refund it in full. That includes a duplicate charge, a charge after you cancelled, or a charge for the wrong plan or amount. If you spot one, email us and we will fix it.

## 5. Founding members

Founding members get the same 30-day guarantee on their first payment. A refund or cancellation ends the founding membership, and the founding rate ends with it, as our Terms of Service explain.

## 6. What we do not refund

- renewal payments, except for the mistakes in Section 4
- the unused part of a billing period after you cancel
- costs from other companies, such as ad spend, fees other platforms charge you, or tools you pay for yourself
- accounts closed because they broke our Terms of Service

## 7. How to ask for a refund

Email hello@buildwithmoxiemo.com from the email address on your MOXIEMO account, with "Refund request" in the subject line. Tell us the plan you are on and the payment you want refunded.

We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Most banks show the refund within 5 to 10 business days.

## 8. Chargebacks

Please email us before you dispute a charge with your bank. Most problems are faster to fix directly. If a chargeback is filed, we may pause your account while the bank reviews it.

## 9. Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any rights you have under the consumer laws where you live. If the law gives you more protection than this policy, the law applies.
