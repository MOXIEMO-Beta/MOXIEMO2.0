# Data Deletion

Last updated October 29, 2026

## The short version

- You can delete your MOXIEMO account and data at any time, from your settings or by email.
- We confirm your request within 3 business days and finish within 30 days of verifying it.
- We keep billing records for 7 years, because tax law requires it.

## 1. Your right to delete your data

You have the right to ask us to delete your personal data from MOXIEMO at any time. This page explains what we delete, what we must keep and how to ask.

## 2. What we delete

When you ask us to delete your data, we permanently delete:

- your account profile: name, email, company and role
- your Discovery answers and conversation history
- your marketing strategy
- all content you created, including posts, emails, campaigns and drafts
- your marketing calendar and planner data
- the authorizations for any social accounts you connected
- your subscriber list data, if you added any

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Download anything you want to keep before you ask.

## 3. What we keep

The law requires us to keep some records after your account is deleted:

- billing and payment records, for 7 years, as tax law requires
- records of legal requests or disputes involving your account
- anonymized, aggregated usage data that cannot identify you

Deleted data can stay in backups for a short time until those backups are replaced.

## 4. How to ask

**In your account.** Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deletion starts right away.

**By email.** Email hello@buildwithmoxiemo.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." Include the email address on your MOXIEMO account, your full name and a short statement that you want your data permanently deleted. We verify your identity before we delete anything.

## 5. How long it takes

We confirm we received your request within 3 business days. We finish deleting your data within 30 days of verifying your identity, and we email you when it is done.

## 6. Connected accounts

If you connected LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram, we remove every stored authorization and token for those accounts. To disconnect completely, also remove MOXIEMO's access in each platform's settings:

- LinkedIn: Settings → Data Privacy → Permitted Services
- Facebook: Settings → Security → Apps and Websites
- Instagram: Settings → Security → Apps and Websites

## 7. Questions

Email hello@buildwithmoxiemo.com. We answer within 3 business days.
