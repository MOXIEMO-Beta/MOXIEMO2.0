## 5. Holds, refunds and chargebacks

Each commission is held for 30 days after the payment it comes from, to cover our 30-day guarantee. After the hold, it becomes payable.

- If a payment is disputed or charged back, we hold its commission until the dispute ends, and cancel it if the dispute goes against us.

If a commission we already paid is later cancelled, we take it out of your next payouts. If there are no payouts within 90 days, you agree to pay it back when we ask.
