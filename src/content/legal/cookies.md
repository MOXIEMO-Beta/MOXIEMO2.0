# Cookie Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 · Last updated October 29, 2026 · Version 1.0

## The short version

- Strictly necessary items keep the site and your sign-in working. They are always on.
- Two small first-party items run for every visitor: a 30-minute visit counter from our host and the version of a button label you see. They do not identify you and are not used for advertising.
- Analytics and marketing cookies, from Microsoft, run only if you allow them.
- You can change your choice anytime with Cookie settings at the bottom of any page. We honor Global Privacy Control.

## 1. What this policy covers

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies on buildwithmoxiemo.com and the MOXIEMO platform. It is part of our Privacy Policy and is issued by KLMG US Ltd, LLC, doing business as MOXIE, Build With MOXIE and MOXIEMO.

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. Browser storage (local storage) works in a similar way but stays on your device and is not sent with every request. Pixels are tiny images in emails that tell us when an email is opened. In this policy, "cookies" covers all three unless we say otherwise.

## 2. Your choices

**2.1 The cookie banner.** On your first visit, our banner lets you accept all, reject all or choose by category. Strictly necessary items run either way.

**2.2 Changing your mind.** Select Cookie settings at the bottom of any page to change your choices. If you withdraw consent, we stop using those cookies from then on and remove Microsoft Clarity cookies from your browser. Withdrawing does not affect what happened before.

**2.3 Global Privacy Control.** If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of analytics and marketing cookies, and of any sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising, for that browser. We record that choice for you automatically.

**2.4 Do Not Track.** Our site does not respond to Do Not Track signals. Use the banner, Cookie settings or Global Privacy Control instead.

**2.5 Your browser.** You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary items, parts of the site, including sign-in, may stop working.

## 3. The kinds of cookies we use

**Strictly necessary.** These keep the site and your account working: sign-in, security, sending you to the current version of the site and remembering your cookie choices. They are always on.

**Site statistics and testing.** Our host, Lovable, counts visits with a cookie that lasts 30 minutes, and we remember which version of a button label you were shown so it stays the same on every page. These run for every visitor. They do not identify you, are not used for advertising and are not shared with advertisers.

**Preferences.** These would remember settings you choose. We do not use any preference cookies right now. If we add one, it will be listed here and will stay off unless you allow it.

**Analytics.** These help us see how visitors use the site so we can fix problems and improve pages. We use Microsoft Clarity, which records interactions such as clicks, scrolls and page movement to create heatmaps and session replays. They are off unless you allow them.

**Marketing.** These help us measure our advertising and campaigns. If you allow them, Microsoft may use data from our site for advertising. They are off unless you allow them.

## 5. Other services that load in your browser

**Google Fonts.** Our pages load fonts from Google. To deliver them, your browser sends your IP address to Google. Google Fonts does not set cookies on our site.

**Stripe.** When you pay, Stripe's checkout sets its own cookies to process the payment and prevent fraud. Stripe's privacy policy covers them.

**Connected platforms.** When you connect an account such as LinkedIn or Meta, you sign in on that platform's own pages, under its own cookie rules.

## 6. Pixels in our emails

Our emails can include a pixel that tells us whether the email was opened and which links were clicked. Most email apps let you block images, which stops the pixel.

## 7. How we record your choice

We keep your choice in your browser for 6 months, or until we change this policy in a way that affects cookies. Then we ask again. To show consent, we also keep a record of each choice in our database: a random ID, your choices, the policy version, the page and the time. We do not store your IP address with that record.

## 8. Changes to this policy

When we add or change a cookie, we update the table above and the date at the top of this page. If a change needs your consent, we ask again through the banner.
