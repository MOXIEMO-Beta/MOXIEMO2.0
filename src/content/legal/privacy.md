# Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 · Last updated October 29, 2026 · Version 2.0

## The short version

- We collect what you give us, such as your account details, your Discovery answers and the content you create, plus basic information about how you use our site.
- We use it to run MOXIEMO, build your strategy and content, bill you and talk to you.
- Your Discovery answers and strategy go to Anthropic's Claude to generate your strategy. We do not let outside AI providers train their models on them without your written consent.
- We do not sell your personal information. Analytics and marketing cookies run only if you allow them, and we honor Global Privacy Control.
- MOXIE text messages are optional. Reply STOP anytime. We never share your mobile number for marketing.
- You can see, correct, download or delete your information. Email hello@buildwithmoxiemo.com.

The short version is a summary. The full policy below is what applies.

## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

KLMG US Ltd, LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company doing business as MOXIE, Build With MOXIE and MOXIEMO ("MOXIEMO," "we," "us" or "our"), runs buildwithmoxiemo.com and the MOXIEMO platform. This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information when you use our website, platform, free tools, MOXIE, our community and our emails and text messages (the "Services").

It covers founding members, beta users, paid members, people who use our free tools, people on our lists, affiliates and visitors.

It does not cover the platforms you connect to MOXIEMO (such as LinkedIn, Meta, TikTok, Google or your email provider), websites we link to, or Stripe, which handles payments under its own privacy policy. Please read their policies too.

**1.1 Your choices about cookies are separate.** Preferences, analytics and marketing cookies need your separate choice in our cookie controls. Visiting the site, creating an account or accepting this policy does not turn them on. The Cookie Policy has the details.

## 2. Information we collect

**2.1 Information you give us.**

- Account details: your name, email address, company or business name, website, role or title, beta access code if you have one, and your password, which is stored encrypted. We never see your password in plain text.
- Founding list and waitlist sign-ups: your first name and email, and anything else the form asks for, such as your role, website, company, how many brands you run, how you heard about us and the plan you are interested in.
- The free growth assessment: the website address you submit and the results we create for it, and your email if you choose to save or receive them.
- Discovery answers: your answers about your business, which can include sensitive business information such as your positioning, your audience, your revenue goals, your brand voice, your current marketing and its results, and any documents or links you share.
- Content you create: posts, emails, campaigns, strategy documents and anything else you write, generate, save, edit or publish in MOXIEMO.
- Messages: what you send us by email, through feedback forms, in support conversations or in our community.
- Payment details: your billing name and address and card details, which Stripe collects and stores. We never store full card numbers.
- Text messages: your mobile number, your opt-in and opt-out records, your MOXIE settings and your replies, when texting is available.
- Affiliate program: your application, your payout details and the tax forms we need to pay you.

**2.2 Information collected automatically.**

- Usage: pages viewed, features used, buttons clicked, time on pages, content generated and session length.
- Device and technical details: IP address, browser and version, operating system, device type, screen size, referring page and language.
- Cookies and browser storage: small files and stored items that keep the site working, count visits, remember your choices and, if you allow them, measure analytics and marketing. The Cookie Policy lists each one.
- Site tests: which version of a page element you were shown, such as a button label. If you sign up, we save that version with your sign-up so we can see which one works better.
- Email activity: whether our emails were opened and links in them were clicked.

**2.3 Information from others.**

- Connected platforms: when you connect an account such as LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram, we receive what you authorize, such as basic profile details, page or account identifiers, and results for content published through MOXIEMO. You can disconnect anytime in your settings. Disconnecting stops our ongoing access but does not erase data we already received.
- Public website content: when you request a free growth assessment, we read the public pages of the website you submit.
- Stripe: confirmation of payments, refunds and disputes.
- Affiliates: if you arrive through an affiliate's link, we record that the referral came from that affiliate.

## 9. How long we keep information

- Account data and Discovery answers: for as long as your account is open. We delete or anonymize them within 30 days after you delete your account, unless the law requires us to keep them.
- Billing and payment records: 7 years, for tax and accounting rules.
- Messages and support records: 3 years, or longer if the law requires.
- Founding list and waitlist entries: until you ask us to remove you, or until we close that list.
- Cookie choice records: as long as we need them to show your consent.
- Usage and analytics data: kept in aggregated, anonymized form for as long as it helps us improve MOXIEMO.

Deleted information can stay in backups for a short time until those backups are replaced. If the law requires us to keep something you asked us to delete, we will tell you what and why.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information, including:

- encryption in transit (TLS) between your device and our servers
- encrypted storage for sensitive data, including Discovery answers and account credentials
- row-level security, so each user can reach only their own data
- access limits, so only people and vendors who need data can reach it
- regular security reviews

No system is completely secure. Use a strong, unique password, and tell us right away at hello@buildwithmoxiemo.com if you think someone has accessed your account.

**10.1 Data breaches.** If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you in MOXIEMO or by email within seven (7) business days of discovering and confirming it, to the extent required and as permitted by law. The notice will describe what happened, the kinds of information involved, what we are doing about it and steps you can take to protect yourself.

## 18. Text messaging (MOXIE texts)

**18.1 What MOXIE texts you.** If you opt in to MOXIE texts, you may receive messages about your content schedule, post approval requests, weekly performance summaries, and reminders to review or approve content. Message frequency depends on your settings and your activity in the app — typically one to five messages per week.

**18.2 How you give consent.** We ask for your consent before sending any texts. We collect consent through an opt-in form in your account settings or at buildwithmoxiemo.com/text. The opt-in checkbox is never pre-ticked. You enter your phone number and tick the box yourself. We confirm consent with a six-digit code sent to that number.

**18.3 Commands you can send.** Reply to any MOXIE text with these keywords:
- **HELP** — we send you a brief description and a link to reach support
- **STOP** — we immediately stop all texts to your number and record your opt-out

**18.4 Cost.** Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply. MOXIEMO does not charge you for texts.

**18.5 Opting out.** You can stop texts at any time by replying STOP to any MOXIE text, or by turning off MOXIE texts in your account settings. We honour opt-out requests immediately. You will not receive any further marketing texts after opting out.

**18.6 Opting in is not required.** Receiving MOXIE texts is entirely optional. You can use MOXIEMO in full without opting in to texts.

**18.7 Your number stays private.** We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your mobile phone number to any third party for their marketing purposes. Your number is used only to send you the texts described in Section 18.1.

**18.8 Carriers.** Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. For questions, contact us at hello@buildwithmoxiemo.com or reply HELP.
